# Visual Simulation

"**A wallet's security is only as strong as its authentication system**."

🛡️ In today's world, wallets either lack strong authentication options or rely on centralized ones.

Here is a **demo** of how Interstellar's decentralized authentication can securely validate any transaction or sensitive operation with the convenience of your daily smartphone:

1. **Choose the device you are currently using**

→ 📱 [Desktop Simulation](https://simu.interstellar.gg/3F60.html)
→ 💻 [Mobile Simulation](https://simu.interstellar.gg/3F60M.html)

2. **Press "Ok"** — you can leave the "participant" field empty

![Visual Simulation Box](/img/Visual_Simulation_Box.png)

3. **Take a screenshot**

4. **Look at your screenshot** ✨

**→** Thanks to the **human persistence of vision**, your eyes and **brain retain a visual impression for about 1/30 of a second** (the exact time depends on the brightness of the image). Interstellar leverages this to generate so-called “visual cryptographic shares” which are displayed at 60–120 frames per second on your device. **This technology will be able to secure next-gen mobile wallets** and eliminate significant attack vectors like fake transactions, overlay attacks, clipper malware, and much more — deeming them ineffective.
