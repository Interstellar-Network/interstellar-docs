# Interstellar Network

Interstellar is a purpose-built deep technology powering **advanced authentication**, **human-centric wallets**, and **mobile applications** across **any blockchain** and **agentic payment network**.

It’s architecture is the result of 20+ years of R&D in the fields of cybersecurity, mobile device security, authentication, and blockchain.

Introducing a new technology that enables **progressive security for sensitive operations on your mobile device** — even if compromised — without additional hardware...

While highly secure, Interstellar’s UX is comparable to a **modern passkey login**, but **without relying on a** [**centralized point of failure**](https://x.com/joenrv/status/1857609397607813171) — enabling both, a frictionless user onboarding + resiliance against state of the art malware attacks.
