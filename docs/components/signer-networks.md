# Signer Networks

**Interstellar's Proof of Human Intent can be verified by highly optimized signer networks, client-owned or decentralized.** These signer networks generate and coordinate one-time [garbled circuits](/proof-of-human-intent/garbled-circuits) that power Interstellar's [dynamic visual cryptography](/proof-of-human-intent/dynamic-visual-cryptography), and are **ultimately responsible for verification and execution of PoHI workflows**.

![Cutting-Edge Blockchain Technology](/img/Cutting_Edge_Blockchain_Technology_Slide.png)

:::info
Interstellar's own, blockchain-based signer network is [currently operating on Testnet](/deployments/blockchains).
:::
