# Mobile Devices

**Security that people actually use has to work on the devices they already carry.** For most people, that means their everyday mobile device, which is where they already approve logins and transactions every day.

**When the stakes are higher, the usual answer is to add another layer of security. [But those layers where not built for the AI era](/proof-of-human-intent/overview):**

- Credential-based layers like passwords, email codes, phone numbers and authenticator apps can be stolen, leaked or misused
- Extra hardware is expensive, needs to be set up, carried around, and can present a single point of failure

![World-Leading Mobile Security](/img/World-Leading_Mobile_Security_Slide.png)

**Interstellar removes those layers entirely. It connects your phone's hardware capabilities, to [purpose-built signer networks](/components/signer-networks)**, so there's nothing to remember, nothing to intercept and nothing extra to carry. **The device you already trust is all you need.**
