# vs Traditional Wallets

| Aspect | Traditional Wallets | Interstellar |
|--------|---------------------|--------------|
| **UX: Setup** | Complex onboarding with seed phrases, passwords, manual backups, and multiple steps. | Instant onboarding, one-tap backup.
| **UX: Daily Use** | Switching devices and wallets can be clunky, with repeated logins and approvals. | Consistent experience across devices with streamlines approvals optimized for mobile.
| **UX: Recovery** | Requires finding and re-entering long recovery phrases. | Seamless one-tap recovery.
| **Security: Exposure** | Single point of failure: enhanced seed phrase and private key exposure; irreversible loss if compromised. | No seed phrases or accessible private keys.
| **Security: Phishing** | Highly vulnerable to phishing, malicious connections/approvals, malware... | Secure, even if wallet or device is fully compromised; no credentials to steal.
