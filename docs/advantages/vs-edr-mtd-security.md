# vs EDR & MTD Security

| Aspect | EDR & MTD | Interstellar |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **Security: Model** | Detects and responds to threats on the device or network level. | Protects from threats on the human intent level. |
| **Security: Scope** | Focuses on malware, intrusion, and policy violations on devices and networks. | Protects authorization and signing — even when the session, agent, screen, or device has already been compromised. | 
| **UX: Setup** | Install agents, configure policies, train users on alerts and responses. | No setups needed or users to train. |
| **UX: Performance** | Agents and scans can slow devices; prompts and blocks can interrupt work. | Doesn't require device protection to counter threats; performance unaffected. |
