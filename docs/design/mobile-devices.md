# Mobile Devices

Mobile devices play a crucial role in Interstellar's architecture. They are the **interaction surface for dynamic human-intents**.

![World-Leading Mobile Security](/img/World-Leading_Mobile_Security_Slide.png)

## Interstellar's Mobile Security Innovation

PoHI **equips your everyday smartphone with next-generation mobile device security** by combining multiple layers of protection in a unique way. Unlike traditional solutions that try to detect malware — which is particularly difficult on mobile devices — this approach **creates a secure environment for critical digital actions that makes tempering attempts visible if your device has been compromised**.

## How It Works

The system uses **multiple strong authentication factors**, all working together:

### 1. Your Device's Hardware Security

Your phone's built-in **Secure Element (SE)** stores a special key that is activated by your biometrics, fingerprint or face. This provides hardware-level security similar to what's found in hardware wallets — but **conveniently integrated into your everyday's mobile device**.

### 2. The Intent Message

When you initiate an intent, you [see exactly what you approve](/proof-of-human-intent/dynamic-visual-cryptography) (amount, d destination...). While this may seem basic, it's **presented in a way that AI or malware cannot silently tamper with**, making it a crucial security factor.

### 3. Dynamic Visual Authorization

**The system creates a unique cryptographic "movie"** that the human eye can see:

- The “movie” is [generated on-chain](/design/interstellar-blockchain) and contains a one-time code
- Each frame is **protected by advanced cryptography** called [garbled circuits](/proof-of-human-intent/garbled-circuits)

:::info
**Upcoming security layers are under development and have yet to be revealed.**
:::

## Protection Against Advanced Threats

This **multi-layered security approach** is effective against sophisticated threats. The [threat model](/proof-of-human-intent/threat-model) covers how Proof of Human Intent protects against each of them:

- Scams and Social Engineering
- Prompt Injection
- Clipper Malware
- Interface Manipulation

## Breakthrough

Unlike [traditional EDRs](/advantages/vs-edr-mtd-security), which struggle on mobile, this approach:

- Is delivered through a **simple integration**
- Requires **no additional hardware or credentials**
- **Focuses protection on critical actions**
- Works **even on compromised devices**

It’s a new paradigm: rather than detecting every threat, it creates a **sealed, verifiable environment for sensitive actions** that remains trustworthy regardless of device integrity.
