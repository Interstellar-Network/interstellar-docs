# Interstellar Blockchain

Interstellar consists of its own **purpose-built blockchain, optimized for verification and execution of PoHI workflows**. The chain generates and coordinates one-time [garbled circuits](/proof-of-human-intent/garbled-circuits) that power the [dynamic visual cryptography](/proof-of-human-intent/dynamic-visual-cryptography) channel, and hosts decentralized signer verification for approved intents.
