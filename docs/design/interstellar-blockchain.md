# Interstellar Blockchain

Interstellar consists of its own **purpose-built Layer 1 blockchain**, responsible for execution and verification of PoHI workflows.

![Cutting-Edge Blockchain Technology](/img/Cutting_Edge_Blockchain_Technology_Slide.png)

The chain generates and coordinates one-time [garbled circuits](/proof-of-human-intent/garbled-circuits) that power the [dynamic visual cryptography](/proof-of-human-intent/dynamic-visual-cryptography) channel, and hosts decentralized signer verification for approved intents.
